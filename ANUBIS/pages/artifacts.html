SACRED ARTIFACTS

Treasures of the gods preserved through millennia by Anubis' eternal grace.

Objects of Divine Purpose

Every artifact found in Egyptian tombs was placed with sacred intention. Shabti figurines, protective amulets, canopic jars, eternal furniture—nothing was random. All was sacred.

These objects bridge two worlds, offering windows into the beliefs and aspirations of those who lived 4,000 years ago.

Categories of Artifacts

Funeral Equipment

Including masks, sarcophagi, and coffins that preserved identity and enabled eternal existence.

Amulets & Talismans

Including the Ankh for eternal life and the Scarab symbolizing resurrection and renewal.

Canopic Jars

Four sacred vessels guarded by the sons of Horus, each protecting different organs of the deceased.

Sacred Texts

The Book of the Dead and sacred inscriptions guiding souls through the Duat.

Tutankhamun's Treasures

The boy king's tomb contained over 5,000 artifacts prepared for his eternal journey. His golden mask remains the most iconic object from ancient Egypt.

Furniture of ebony and ivory, weapons inlaid with precious stones, linen garments, cosmetics still fragrant—all prepared for eternal continuation.
