YOUR SACRED JOURNEY

The path through the Duat. The trials you must face. The eternity that awaits.

The Duat: Realm of the Dead

Death was not an ending—it was a transformation. The soul entered the Duat, a hidden dimension where it faced twelve hours of night, each testing the soul's virtue and knowledge.

The Twelve Hours of the Night

First Hour

The soul enters and the gates open. Guardians appear, their names inscribed in sacred texts. Knowledge is the first key.

Middle Hours

The soul navigates lakes of fire and serpents. These test the soul's resolve. Only the pure of heart can pass.

The Weighing

The climax. The heart is weighed against the feather of truth. Anubis observes. This is the moment of absolute truth.

Final Hours

If found worthy, the soul ascends and joins Ra's eternal journey across the sky. True peace and eternal life.

Anubis: Your Guide Through the Duat

Anubis does not judge—he guides. He knows the way through the Duat. His jackal senses sniff out truth from deception. When you stand before the scales, Anubis steadies your heart and gives you courage to face judgment.

He is the eternal witness, keeper of secrets, and guardian of the boundary between life and death. Trust in Anubis is trust in eternity itself.

Seek wisdom to prepare for your eternal journey.
