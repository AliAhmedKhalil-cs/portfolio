Practical Information

Visa & Entry

  • Most nationalities can obtain a tourist visa on arrival
  • Passport valid for 6+ months from entry date
  • eVisa available online (90 days)

Currency & Payments

  • Currency: Egyptian Pound (EGP)
  • ATMs widely available in major cities
  • Credit cards accepted in tourist areas

Safety & Health

  • Tourist areas are generally safe and well-patrolled
  • Drink bottled water; avoid tap water
  • Sun protection essential; temperatures can reach 40°C

Best Time to Visit

  • October - April: ideal weather (20-25°C)
  • May - September: hot, fewer tourists
  • Ramadan: altered business hours
